# Fact Desk content-use policy

The Fact Desk is a briefing and source-discovery product, not a replacement
for publishers' reporting.

## Default treatment

- Publisher headlines may be displayed as attributed, linked metadata.
- RSS descriptions, ledes, article bodies, photographs, graphics, and video
  are analysis-only and are not republished.
- Public summaries and "what happened" copy must be written by The Fact Desk
  from headline-level facts, evidence metadata, and independent significance
  templates.
- Single-source claims remain explicitly attributed. Multiple publications
  covering the same originating claim do not automatically make it confirmed.
- Quoted language is used only when editorially necessary, kept brief, clearly
  attributed, and linked to the underlying source.

## Licensed exceptions

A feed description may be displayed only when its feed configuration includes
a reviewed syndication licence URL expressly allowing that reuse. Attribution,
modification, commercial-use, territory, and expiry conditions must be recorded
before enabling the exception.

## Accuracy and viewpoint integrity

The content firewall must not sanitize a source's allegation into an established
fact or soften politically material language without disclosure. The original
headline attribution and source links stay visible. Confidence, urgency,
viewpoint representation, and corroboration remain separate signals.

## Operations

- Use official feeds and public endpoints; do not bypass paywalls or access
  controls.
- Maintain a prompt correction and takedown channel.
- Recheck source terms before introducing advertising, memberships, or other
  commercial use.
- Legal review is required before enabling any licensed-summary exception at
  scale.
