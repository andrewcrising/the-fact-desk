# Fact Desk content-use policy

The Fact Desk is a briefing and source-discovery product, not a replacement
for publishers' reporting.

## Default treatment

- Publisher headlines may be displayed as attributed, linked metadata.
- RSS descriptions, ledes, article bodies, photographs, graphics, and video
  are analysis-only and are not republished.
- Public summaries and "what happened" copy must be independently generated by
  The Fact Desk from a structured evidence bundle. Every material claim records
  the source evidence that supports it before publication.
- Single-source claims remain explicitly attributed. Multiple publications
  covering the same originating claim do not automatically make it confirmed.
- Quoted language is used only when editorially necessary, kept brief, clearly
  attributed, and linked to the underlying source.

## Licensed exceptions

A feed description may be displayed only when its feed configuration includes
a reviewed syndication licence URL expressly allowing that reuse. Attribution,
modification, commercial-use, territory, and expiry conditions must be recorded
before enabling the exception.

## Accuracy and viewpoint integrity

The content firewall must not sanitize a source's allegation into an established
fact or soften politically material language without disclosure. The original
headline attribution and source links stay visible. Confidence, urgency,
viewpoint representation, and corroboration remain separate signals.

## Evidence-synthesis publication gate

- Feed descriptions remain private research inputs and are never attached to a
  public story object or browser response.
- A synopsis must state what happened and why it matters in fresh language,
  using only facts present in its supplied evidence bundle.
- Single-source accounts must identify the reporting publisher in the public
  synopsis. Allegations, forecasts, opinions, and disputed assertions remain
  attributed even when several publishers repeat them.
- Generated numbers must appear in the source evidence, and every material
  claim must map to at least one valid source index.
- Copy with URLs, markup, generic filler, unsupported details, or excessive
  phrase overlap with source prose is rejected before publication.
- Phrase-overlap thresholds are engineering guardrails, not a legal safe harbor.
  A failed generation or validation check falls back to clearly labeled,
  attribution-only source discovery rather than publishing uncertain prose.
- Generation model, time, source fingerprint, source count, claim count, and
  provider generation identifier (when available) are retained for audit.
- A validated briefing is cached against the fingerprint of its complete
  evidence set. Unchanged evidence reuses the same reviewed output; new or
  changed evidence receives a new synthesis and audit record.

## Operations

- Use official feeds and public endpoints; do not bypass paywalls or access
  controls.
- Maintain a prompt correction and takedown channel.
- Recheck source terms before introducing advertising, memberships, or other
  commercial use.
- Legal review is required before enabling any licensed-summary exception at
  scale.
- Legal review is required before commercial launch; this policy reduces risk
  but does not guarantee that a use is non-infringing or otherwise lawful.
