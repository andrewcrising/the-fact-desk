# Live evidence placement policy

Automatic public placement follows these rules:

1. Multi-source coverage leads the live feed.
2. Primary-source-only updates are shown separately and are authoritative only for what the issuing institution published or announced.
3. Ordinary single-newsroom items remain Incoming Signals until additional support appears.
4. A single-newsroom item cannot become the automatic Top Signal lead.
5. Manual editorial overrides remain available for exceptional cases and should be used deliberately.

Multiple publishers improve support but do not, by themselves, prove that every reported claim is independently verified.
